## Overview

KAROSERI is an enterprise auto body repair and modification management system (*Bengkel Karoseri & Perbaikan Bodi*). It coordinates end-to-end workshop operations from intake estimation to 3-element job order costing, segregated 2-pillar profit analysis (package assembly margin vs granular non-package margin), production, quality control, warehouse inventory control, billing, periodic operational expenses (OPEX) with dynamic category management, executive corporate financial intelligence, comprehensive double-entry accounting (*Buku Besar & Kas Akuntansi Model T*), and unified financial reconciliation (RAB Perubahan vs Kasir Billing) across six core roles:
- **Owner / Administrator** (Executive oversight, 6-card KPI analytics, active SPK real-time financial metrics monitoring table [Total HPP, Total Tagihan, Realisasi Biaya Aktual & Varian Pagu], Buku Besar & Kas Akuntansi Model T [Aktiva vs Pasiva], corporate P&L, CVP sales target solver, financial sanity audits, master settings, dynamic expense categories, audit trails, backup/restore, owner simulation reset, and one-click itemized package vs granular profit drill-down modal)
- **Service Advisor (SA)** (Vehicle intake with photo inspection & vehicle model tracking, SPK creation, 3-element RAB estimation with Origin / Package tracking, proportional package BOM explosion, consolidated **RAB Perubahan** audit tab linking initial contract + approved change orders with warehouse fulfillment progress, 3-group profit summary [Grup A: Margin Khusus Paket Modifikasi / Assembly, Grup B: Margin Pekerjaan Granular / Non-Paket, Grup C: Rekapitulasi Total Margin & Laba Proyek] with package labor isolation, XLSX/PDF exports, itemized change order / amendment management with assembly packages insertion, multi-instance package support, segregated 4-pillar margin badges, zero-initialized default WBS allocations, and material requisitions)
- **Foreman / Mandor** (7-stage WBS tracking, borongan worker assignments with completion deadlines from RAB & Change Orders isolated by unique item IDs to prevent false shared exclusion, photo uploads, floor terminal actual overhead logging with dynamic presets, and pending material inspection)
- **Warehouse Officer / Petugas Gudang** (Granular item-level material requisition approvals with Gate 2 Material-Only budget guard, formatted document and package source origin resolution, goods issue against RAB quota limits, stock rollback, restock audit logging, and ascending WBS sequence sorting)
- **Cashier / Kasir** (Down payment recording with receipt print & history, unified customer billing calculator, final invoice settlement with itemized Change Order breakdown accordion, HPP Change Order integration into Internal Cost modal panel, live gross profit margin indicators, Buku Besar & Kas non-SPK logging, handover billing clearance gate, BAST handover with automated vehicle release timestamping)
- **Customer (Public)** (Read-only real-time SPK progress timeline via `/tracking/:spk_no` protected by a 7-day post-BAST expiration boundary transitioning into a branded customer appreciation card)

## Database Schema & Architecture

### Tables (31 tables in `public` schema)
1. `profiles`: User accounts linked with Supabase Auth (`user_id`, `role`, `full_name`).
2. `spk`: Core job orders (`id`, `spk_no`, `customer_name`, `license_plate`, `vehicle_model`, `vehicle_brand`, `chassis_no`, `engine_no`, `status`, `released_at`, etc.).
3. `spk_assets`: Intake and damage photo gallery records with Cloudinary URLs.
4. `spk_amendments`: Change order / amendment logs.
5. `materials`: Inventory catalog with dual pricing (HPP vs selling price) and item classification (`MATERIAL`, `LABOR`, `OVERHEAD`).
6. `rab_estimations`: Top-level RAB estimate per SPK.
7. `rab_items`: Itemized RAB allocations with `item_type`, WBS stage mappings, frequency, prices, and `package_name`.
8. `inventory_transactions`: Historical stock ledger entries.
9. `wbs_checklists`: 7-stage production checklist records.
10. `qc_inspections`: Quality control pass/fail checklists.
11. `invoices`: Customer billing and invoice settlements.
12. `workshop_settings`: General workshop metadata, logos, letterheads.
13. `payments`: Payment receipts (down payments & final payments).
14. `material_requisitions`: Requisition headers submitted by SA or Mandor with status (`PENDING`, `PARTIALLY_ISSUED`, `APPROVED`, `COMPLETED`, `REJECTED`).
15. `material_requisition_items`: Itemized requisition requests with status (`PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`), stage mappings, and Change Order linkages.
16. `spk_borongan`: Borongan subcontract labor assignments to workers from RAB and Change Orders with written completion deadline (`deadline_date`) and unique line item linkage (`rab_item_id`, `change_order_item_id`).
17. `product_packages`: Predefined modification/repair bundles.
18. `package_items`: Bill of Materials (BOM) items for packages.
19. `inventory_audit_logs`: User-attributed audit log for price and stock edits.
20. `material_issues`: Physical warehouse goods issued against approved requisitions.
21. `mandor_requisitions`: Compatibility / direct mandor requisition requests.
22. `material_returns`: Unused materials returned back to stock.
23. `requisition_items`: Requisition line items.
24. `change_orders`: Header for customer change orders / scope modifications.
25. `change_order_items`: Itemized 3-element change order additions with `package_source_id`, `package_instance_id`, and `package_name`.
26. `spk_overhead_logs`: Floor terminal actual factory overhead cost logs recorded by Mandors.
27. `operating_expense_categories`: Legacy master OPEX categories.
28. `operating_expenses`: Periodic operational expense ledger entries with `category_id` FK.
29. `expense_categories`: Dynamic master OPEX categories (`id`, `name UNIQUE`, `description`, `is_active`, timestamps).
30. `initial_balances`: Starting financial baseline for workshop double-entry accounting (`cash_bank`, `inventory_value`, `equipment_value`, `supplies_value`, `payables`, generated stored `initial_equity`, `is_locked`).
31. `cash_ledger_transactions`: Non-SPK cash book for capital injections, loans, equipment purchases, consumables, prive withdrawals, and debt repayments.

- `view_inventory_valuation`: Total warehouse stock value at cost (HPP) and market selling price.

### Stored Procedures / RPC Functions (13 functions)
- `reset_all_simulation_transactions`: Owner-exclusive procedure wiping all transactional simulation data (payments, invoices, cash_ledger_transactions, inventory_transactions, material requisitions, goods issues/returns, wbs checklists, qc inspections, change orders, rab items/estimations, and spk) while preserving catalog master data, setting material inventory stock to 100, and unlocking initial balances.
- `approve_single_requisition_item`: Atomically approves an individual material line item, deducts inventory stock, writes to `inventory_transactions`, and auto-progresses parent requisition status to `PARTIALLY_ISSUED` or `COMPLETED`.
- `reject_single_requisition_item`: Atomically rejects an individual material line item and evaluates overall requisition status (`REJECTED`, `PARTIALLY_ISSUED`, `COMPLETED`).
- `get_financial_executive_summary`: Generates 6-card executive financial KPIs with strict non-draft exclusions.
- `audit_financial_sanity_check`: Automated financial diagnostic engine scanning for negative margins, budget overruns, and billing anomalies.
- `void_material_issue` / `void_material_issues`: Reverses material issue transactions and restores inventory quantities safely.
- `check_spk_cancellation`: Validates that SPK has no payments or issued materials before permitting cancellation.
- `check_spk_completion_billing_status`: Verifies invoice settlement before allowing project completion.
- `lock_spk_pricing_on_approval`: Freezes RAB unit prices upon project approval.
- `sync_change_order_to_spk_amendments`: Mirrors approved change orders into the legacy amendments ledger.
- `sync_spk_vehicle_ident_columns`: Synchronizes vehicle identity fields across records.
- `wbs_category_to_stage`: Normalizes text stage descriptions into canonical 1..7 stage integers.
- `handle_new_user`: Automatically creates profile entry on Supabase auth user sign-up.

### Database Triggers (8 triggers)
- `trg_enforce_handover_billing_lock`: Hard database constraint preventing SPK from being marked `COMPLETED` if invoice is unpaid.
- `trg_check_spk_cancellation`: Rejects cancellation attempts if goods have been issued or down payment received.
- `trg_lock_spk_pricing`: Locks RAB pricing once an SPK transitions to `APPROVED` or `ACTIVE`.
- `trg_sync_change_orders`: Triggers change order synchronization on approval.
- `trg_sync_spk_vehicle_ident`: Synchronizes vehicle identity columns on update.
- `on_auth_user_created`: Triggers profile creation on new auth user creation.

## Conventions

- **Naming:** PascalCase for React components and pages, camelCase for services, hooks, and utilities.
- **Architecture:** Layered modular structure (`components/`, `pages/`, `services/`, `context/`, `hooks/`, `lib/`, `types/`, `utils/`).

- **Job Order Costing:** 3-element costing convention (Direct Material, Direct Labor / Borongan, Factory Overhead / FOH) paired with Segregated Profit Analysis (Package vs Granular) and periodic OPEX reconciliation.
- **Double-Entry Accounting:** Model T Balance Sheet convention (Left: Aktiva vs Right: Pasiva) with `Total Aktiva == Total Pasiva` mathematical balance validation.
