## Overview

KAROSERI is an enterprise auto body repair and modification management system (*Bengkel Karoseri & Perbaikan Bodi*). It coordinates end-to-end workshop operations from intake estimation to 3-element job order costing, production, quality control, warehouse inventory control, billing, periodic operational expenses (OPEX) with dynamic category management, executive corporate financial intelligence, and comprehensive double-entry accounting (*Buku Besar & Kas Akuntansi Model T*) across six core roles:
- **Owner / Administrator** (Executive oversight, 6-card KPI analytics, active SPK real-time financial metrics monitoring table [Total HPP, Total Tagihan, Realisasi Biaya Aktual & Varian Pagu], Buku Besar & Kas Akuntansi Model T [Aktiva vs Pasiva], corporate P&L, CVP sales target solver, financial sanity audits, master settings, dynamic expense categories, audit trails, backup/restore)
- **Service Advisor (SA)** (Vehicle intake with photo inspection & vehicle model tracking, SPK creation, 3-element RAB estimation, itemized change order / amendment management with assembly packages insertion, material requisitions)
- **Foreman / Mandor** (7-stage WBS tracking, borongan worker assignments with completion deadlines from RAB & Change Orders, photo uploads, floor terminal actual overhead logging with presets, pending material inspection)
- **Warehouse Officer / Petugas Gudang** (Granular item-level material requisition approvals with Gate 2 Material-Only budget guard, goods issue against RAB quota limits, stock rollback, restock audit logging)
- **Cashier / Kasir** (Down payment recording with receipt print & history, final invoice settlement with itemized Change Order breakdown, Buku Besar & Kas non-SPK logging, handover billing clearance gate, BAST handover with automated vehicle release timestamping)
- **Customer (Public)** (Read-only real-time SPK progress timeline via `/tracking/:spk_no` protected by a 7-day post-BAST expiration boundary transitioning into a branded customer appreciation card)

## Data Flow

1. **User Authentication & Session Initialization:**
   - [`AuthContext.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/context/AuthContext.tsx) resolves user session and fetches profile metadata from `profiles` table.
   - [`App.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/App.tsx) routes the user to the role-specific dashboard.
2. **Work Order Lifecycle:**
   - **Intake:** SA creates SPK ([`SpkForm.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/SpkForm.tsx)) recording vehicle model, brand, chassis, license plate, uploads initial condition photos to Cloudinary, and prepares 3-element RAB estimation ([`RabCalculator.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/RabCalculator.tsx)).
   - **Approval & DP:** Customer approves; Kasir records Down Payment ([`DownPaymentModal.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/DownPaymentModal.tsx)), with printable DP receipt ([`DpReceiptPrintModal.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/finance/DpReceiptPrintModal.tsx)), transitioning SPK from `DRAFT` / `PENDING_DP` to `ACTIVE`.
   - **Owner / Executive Active SPK Monitoring:** Owner observes real-time financial health on `/dashboard` via [`ActiveSpkTable.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/ActiveSpkTable.tsx) powered by [`dashboardService.ts`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/services/dashboardService.ts), reviewing Total HPP (RAB modal + approved CO modal), Total Tagihan (contract revenue + approved CO revenue), Realisasi Biaya Aktual (actual materials issued + borongan labor + recorded overhead), and real-time variance badges (Overbudget vs Sisa Pagu).
   - **Production & Mandor Assignment:** Mandor assigns workers ([`SpkBoronganPanel.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/SpkBoronganPanel.tsx)) with written completion deadlines (`deadline_date`) pulling labor items from both RAB and approved Change Orders, checks off 7 WBS stages ([`WbsChecklist.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/WbsChecklist.tsx)), records actual FOH bay/tool costs via [`SpkOverheadModal.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/SpkOverheadModal.tsx) using dynamic RAB/CO presets, and documents progress photos ([`WbsGalleryUploader.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/WbsGalleryUploader.tsx)).
   - **Material Requisition & Issue (Granular & Gate 2 Guarded):** SA/Mandor requests materials via [`MaterialRequisitionForm.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/MaterialRequisitionForm.tsx); Warehouse approves individually or in batches via [`RequisitionApproval.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/RequisitionApproval.tsx) with atomic RPC procedures (`approve_single_requisition_item`, `reject_single_requisition_item`), enforced by Gate 2 Material-Only budget guard ([`gate2BudgetValidation.ts`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/utils/gate2BudgetValidation.ts)), automatically transitioning tickets to `PARTIALLY_ISSUED` or `COMPLETED`. Warehouse issues items against RAB quota limits via [`GoodsIssueForm.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/GoodsIssueForm.tsx).
   - **Change Orders & Assembly Packages:** Unforeseen work or product packages (assembly lists) are submitted via [`AmendmentManager.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/AmendmentManager.tsx) through [`ChangeOrderPackageModal.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/ChangeOrderPackageModal.tsx) and [`packageExplosion.ts`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/utils/packageExplosion.ts). Approved Change Orders propagate dynamically to Mandor Borongan guides, Mandor Overhead presets, Owner Active SPK financial table, and Kasir final billing.
   - **Quality Control:** QC Officer conducts final inspection ([`QcInspectionForm.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/QcInspectionForm.tsx)) to set `PASS` status.
   - **Settlement & Handover Gate:** Kasir reviews balance with itemized Change Order breakdowns in [`KasirDashboard.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/pages/KasirDashboard.tsx). Handover gate blocks BAST generation until invoice status is strictly `LUNAS`. Database trigger `trg_enforce_handover_billing_lock` rolls back any query attempting to mark SPK `COMPLETED` if unpaid. Once paid, signed BAST is issued ([`BastModal.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/BastModal.tsx)), stamping `released_at = NOW()`.
   - **Public Tracking & Privacy Expiration:** Vehicle owner tracks progress in real-time via `/tracking/:spk_no`. After 7 days post-BAST, technical WBS and fabrication photos are masked and replaced by an official branded customer appreciation card with post-handover warranty guidance.
3. **General Ledger & T-Model Balance Sheet (Buku Besar & Kas):**
   - Initial workshop balances configured via [`InitialBalanceModal.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/accounting/InitialBalanceModal.tsx) persist to `public.initial_balances` with generated stored equity.
   - Non-SPK transactions (capital injections, debt, equipment/supplies purchases, prive) are logged in `public.cash_ledger_transactions` via [`CashTransactionModal.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/components/accounting/CashTransactionModal.tsx).
   - [`generalLedgerService.ts`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/services/generalLedgerService.ts) reconciles Aktiva (Cash, Receivables, Warehouse Inventory, WIP, Supplies, Equipment) against Pasiva (Payables, Initial Equity, Capital Injections, Net Profit, Prive, New Equity), proving the double-entry accounting identity `Total Aktiva == Total Pasiva` on [`GeneralLedgerPage.tsx`](file:///c:/KAROSERI/src/pages/GeneralLedgerPage.tsx).
4. **Financial Reporting & Corporate Intelligence:**
   - Database views aggregate live job profitability (`view_spk_job_profitability`), WBS cost variances (`view_wbs_cost_variance`), inventory valuation (`view_inventory_valuation`), aging receivables (`view_project_receivables`), and corporate monthly P&L (`view_corporate_monthly_pnl`).
   - Dynamic OPEX categories table `public.expense_categories` feeds modal logging with in-place category management (`ManageExpenseCategoriesModal.tsx`).
   - All financial views and executive RPC summaries strictly exclude unapproved / preliminary SPKs (`DRAFT` and `PENDING_DP`).
   - Executive KPI cards decouple Material Cost, Borongan Mandor cost, and Factory Overhead allocation (FOH) from Total COGS across a 6-column grid.
   - Period-based Corporate P&L consolidates workshop gross profit with operating expenses (OPEX) to track monthly EBIT and net profit margin.
   - CVP Optimization Solver computes break-even volume, target profit requirements, and safety margins.
   - Export engine produces executive Excel spreadsheets and formal printable PDF reports.

## Database Schema & Architecture

### Tables (31 tables in `public` schema)
1. `profiles`: User accounts linked with Supabase Auth (`user_id`, `role`, `full_name`).
2. `spk`: Core job orders (`id`, `spk_no`, `customer_name`, `license_plate`, `vehicle_model`, `vehicle_brand`, `chassis_no`, `engine_no`, `status`, `released_at`, etc.).
3. `spk_assets`: Intake and damage photo gallery records with Cloudinary URLs.
4. `spk_amendments`: Change order / amendment logs.
5. `materials`: Inventory catalog with dual pricing (HPP vs selling price) and item classification (`MATERIAL`, `LABOR`, `OVERHEAD`).
6. `rab_estimations`: Top-level RAB estimate per SPK.
7. `rab_items`: Itemized RAB allocations with `item_type`, WBS stage mappings, frequency, and prices.
8. `inventory_transactions`: Historical stock ledger entries.
9. `wbs_checklists`: 7-stage production checklist records.
10. `qc_inspections`: Quality control pass/fail checklists.
11. `invoices`: Customer billing and invoice settlements.
12. `workshop_settings`: General workshop metadata, logos, letterheads.
13. `payments`: Payment receipts (down payments & final payments).
14. `material_requisitions`: Requisition headers submitted by SA or Mandor with status (`PENDING`, `PARTIALLY_ISSUED`, `APPROVED`, `COMPLETED`, `REJECTED`).
15. `material_requisition_items`: Itemized requisition requests with status (`PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`), stage mappings, and Change Order linkages.
16. `spk_borongan`: Borongan subcontract labor assignments to workers from RAB and Change Orders with written completion deadline (`deadline_date`).
17. `product_packages`: Predefined modification/repair bundles.
18. `package_items`: Bill of Materials (BOM) items for packages.
19. `inventory_audit_logs`: User-attributed audit log for price and stock edits.
20. `material_issues`: Physical warehouse goods issued against approved requisitions.
21. `mandor_requisitions`: Compatibility / direct mandor requisition requests.
22. `material_returns`: Unused materials returned back to stock.
23. `requisition_items`: Requisition line items.
24. `change_orders`: Header for customer change orders / scope modifications.
25. `change_order_items`: Itemized 3-element change order additions.
26. `spk_overhead_logs`: Floor terminal actual factory overhead cost logs recorded by Mandors.
27. `operating_expense_categories`: Legacy master OPEX categories.
28. `operating_expenses`: Periodic operational expense ledger entries with `category_id` FK.
29. `expense_categories`: Dynamic master OPEX categories (`id`, `name UNIQUE`, `description`, `is_active`, timestamps).
30. `initial_balances`: Starting financial baseline for workshop double-entry accounting (`cash_bank`, `inventory_value`, `equipment_value`, `supplies_value`, `payables`, generated stored `initial_equity`, `is_locked`).
31. `cash_ledger_transactions`: Non-SPK cash book for capital injections, loans, equipment purchases, consumables, prive withdrawals, and debt repayments.

### Views (8 views)
- `view_spk_job_profitability`: Live 3-element job order costing and profit margin analysis (excludes DRAFT/PENDING_DP).
- `view_wbs_cost_variance`: WBS stage budget vs actual cost tracking across 3 cost pillars.
- `view_inventory_valuation`: Total warehouse stock value at cost (HPP) and market selling price.
- `view_project_receivables`: Outstanding customer invoices and aging analysis.
- `view_corporate_monthly_pnl`: Consolidated workshop P&L by month combining gross margins with periodic OPEX.
- `spk_orders`: Compatibility view alias for SPK.
- `material_issues`: View for issued material queries.
- `final_billing`: Billing calculation helper view.

### Stored Procedures / RPC Functions (13 functions)
- `reset_all_simulation_transactions`: Owner-exclusive procedure wiping all transactional simulation data (payments, invoices, cash_ledger_transactions, inventory_transactions, material requisitions, goods issues/returns, wbs checklists, qc inspections, change orders, rab items/estimations, and spk) while preserving catalog master data, setting material inventory stock to 100, and unlocking initial balances.
- `approve_single_requisition_item`: Atomically approves an individual material line item, deducts inventory stock, writes to `inventory_transactions`, and auto-progresses parent requisition status to `PARTIALLY_ISSUED` or `COMPLETED`.
- `reject_single_requisition_item`: Atomically rejects an individual material line item and evaluates overall requisition status (`REJECTED`, `PARTIALLY_ISSUED`, `COMPLETED`).
- `get_financial_executive_summary`: Generates 6-card executive financial KPIs with strict non-draft exclusions.
- `audit_financial_sanity_check`: Automated financial diagnostic engine scanning for negative margins, budget overruns, and billing anomalies.
- `void_material_issue` / `void_material_issues`: Reverses material issue transactions and restores inventory quantities safely.
- `check_spk_cancellation`: Validates that SPK has no payments or issued materials before permitting cancellation.
- `check_spk_completion_billing_status`: Verifies invoice settlement before allowing project completion.
- `lock_spk_pricing_on_approval`: Freezes RAB unit prices upon project approval.
- `sync_change_order_to_spk_amendments`: Mirrors approved change orders into the legacy amendments ledger.
- `sync_spk_vehicle_ident_columns`: Synchronizes vehicle identity fields across records.
- `wbs_category_to_stage`: Normalizes text stage descriptions into canonical 1..7 stage integers.
- `handle_new_user`: Automatically creates profile entry on Supabase auth user sign-up.

### Database Triggers (8 triggers)
- `trg_enforce_handover_billing_lock`: Hard database constraint preventing SPK from being marked `COMPLETED` if invoice is unpaid.
- `trg_check_spk_cancellation`: Rejects cancellation attempts if goods have been issued or down payment received.
- `trg_lock_spk_pricing`: Locks RAB pricing once an SPK transitions to `APPROVED` or `ACTIVE`.
- `trg_sync_change_orders`: Triggers change order synchronization on approval.
- `trg_sync_spk_vehicle_ident`: Synchronizes vehicle identity columns on update.
- `on_auth_user_created`: Triggers profile creation on new auth user creation.
- `trg_insert_final_billing`: Maintains billing status consistency.
- `trg_insert_material_issues`: Audits and decrements inventory on issuance.

## Conventions

- **Naming:** PascalCase for React components and pages, camelCase for services, hooks, and utilities.
- **Architecture:** Layered modular structure (`components/`, `pages/`, `services/`, `context/`, `hooks/`, `lib/`, `types/`, `utils/`).
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS v4 utility classes paired with custom design tokens in `index.css`.
- **Database Access:** Supabase client with PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) enforcement, database triggers, and specialized database views for heavy analytics.
- **Job Order Costing:** 3-element costing convention (Direct Material, Direct Labor / Borongan, Factory Overhead / FOH) with periodic OPEX reconciliation.
- **Double-Entry Accounting:** Model T Balance Sheet convention (Left: Aktiva vs Right: Pasiva) with `Total Aktiva == Total Pasiva` mathematical balance validation.
